## Download list (paper PDFs) — surface finish / roughness / optical coupling

Last update: 2025-12-21

### Notes

- This list is meant to be **clicked and downloaded in Chrome** under your campus access.
- Some publisher sites (e.g. WoS / IOP) may trigger **CAPTCHA** for automated browsing; if that happens, open the same DOI link in your normal browser and download manually.
- For IEEE papers: prefer downloading from **IEEE Xplore “PDF”** on the document page.
